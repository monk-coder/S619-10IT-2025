Once upon a time, there was a small language model.
It learned to predict the next token in a sequence.
The model was trained on many examples of text.
After training, it could generate new sentences.
This is the story of a mini-LLM.

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
Pack my box with five dozen liquor jugs.
How vexingly quick daft zebras jump!

Hello, world! This is a test of the emergency broadcast system.
If this were a real emergency, you would be instructed where to go.
This is only a test.

In a hole in the ground there lived a hobbit.
Not a nasty, dirty, wet hole, but a comfortable hobbit-hole.
