This is a sample text corpus for training BPE tokenizer.
It contains multiple lines of English text.
Each line is treated as a separate document during training.
BPE tokenizer learns subword units from character-level initialization.
Rare words are split into frequent subword components.
For example supercalifragilisticexpialidocious might be split into pieces.
The algorithm iteratively merges the most frequent adjacent pairs.
After training we can encode any text into token IDs.
Decoding should perfectly reconstruct the original text.
This guarantees lossless tokenization for NLP pipelines.
Machine learning models benefit from subword tokenization.
It handles unknown words gracefully by composing from known subwords.
Unicode characters are supported through UTF-8 encoding.
Punctuation and numbers are treated as separate tokens.
